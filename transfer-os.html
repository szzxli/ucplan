A note on TAG

Six UC campuses participate in the Transfer Admission Guarantee (TAG): UC Davis, UC Irvine, UC Merced, UC Riverside, UC Santa Barbara, and UC Santa Cruz. UCLA, UC Berkeley, and UC San Diego do not offer TAG. Transfer-OS shows you the path either way — TAG or not, the coursework still has to line up.
