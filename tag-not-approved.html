If your TAG was not approved

A checklist for California community college students whose Transfer Admission Guarantee was denied or voided. Facts below are copied from UC and campus admissions pages. If a line here disagrees with those pages, the official page controls.

Last checked: 2026-11-18

The fall 2027 UC application filing period is October 1 through November 30, 2026. The dates page does not list a general filing date after November 30. A TAG is not the UC application.

UC Admissions: Dates and deadlines

What a TAG denial does and does not mean

  • It does not close the regular UC application. The TAG matrix says transfer students do not have to participate in TAG to be considered for admission, and that students without a TAG apply during the regular filing period. The same matrix says about two-thirds of transfer students admitted to UC do not have a TAG.
  • It does mean the guarantee is not in place. Meeting minimum UC transfer requirements is still required. A regular review is not a guarantee, and nothing on this site promises admission.
  • You can apply to more than the TAG campus. UC Davis states you may submit a TAG to only one of the six TAG campuses, and you may apply for general admission to as many UC campuses as you wish. Pathways+ names the six TAG campuses as all undergraduate campuses except UC Berkeley, UCLA, and UC San Diego.
  • If the TAG was never filed, the UC TAG page says you can still apply during the regular period, October 1–November 30 for fall. An approved TAG still requires that same UC application, for the same major at the TAG campus.

Sources: UC TAG matrix (PDF, accurate as of June 9, 2026) · UC TAG overview · UC Davis TAG FAQ · Pathways+

What to recheck

These are the shared fall 2027 TAG criteria on the UC TAG matrix. Campus minimums are higher, and some majors add preparation courses. Use ASSIST for the courses at your college.

  1. GPA. Compare your UC-transferable GPA with the campus minimum on the TAG matrix. Minimums on this site are listed on the TAG GPA by campus page, taken from that matrix. A campus page can add a later checkpoint. UC Irvine, for example, requires 3.4 by the end of fall 2026 and says to maintain 3.4.
  2. Major preparation. The matrix says you must meet campus criteria, and several majors require specific courses or a major-preparation GPA. ASSIST is the place UC tells community college students to check transferable courses and major agreements.
  3. Seven-course pattern, not IGETC by default. By spring 2027 the matrix requires the rest of the seven-course pattern: two English (UC-E), one math (UC-M), and four courses from at least two of arts and humanities (UC-H), social and behavioral sciences (UC-B), and physical and biological sciences (UC-S). At least one UC-E and one UC-M are due by fall 2026. UC says a completed IGETC or Cal-GETC may already satisfy that pattern. Cal-GETC is not itself a UC admission requirement. The matrix says IGETC or Cal-GETC is not required for TAG except at UC Merced for Public Health.
  4. Units. At TAG submission (end of summer 2026): at least 30 semester or 45 quarter UC-transferable units. By the end of spring 2027: 60 semester or 90 quarter units, at least 30 semester or 45 quarter of them at a California community college, and no more than 19 UC-transferable units in that spring term (or last regular term) to reach 60. The 80 semester / 120 quarter cap applies after lower-division limits. The matrix says students with only community college coursework and AP, IB, or A-level credit do not exceed that cap.
  5. Grades and standing. No grade lower than C in the current fall, winter, and spring terms, and good academic standing. UC’s basic transfer rule is a C or better in each required course, or Pass if the college counts Pass as a C.
  6. Same major, full coursework, and the UC application. The matrix requires the UC application from October 1 to November 30, 2026, with the TAG major matching the application major for that campus, and a Transfer Academic Update by January 31, 2027. Report every college course. The matrix says students are responsible for reporting coursework accurately.
  7. Who cannot get a TAG at any campus: someone who already has a bachelor’s or higher degree; someone who attended a UC and wants to return to that same campus; someone who left a UC not in good standing; someone still in high school when the TAG was submitted.

Deadlines after a fall 2027 TAG

  • UC application for fall 2027: submit between October 1 and November 30, 2026. The application opens August 1. Submit one application per term. The dates page does not list a filing date after November 30, 2026.
  • UC Irvine’s appeals page describes a late-application appeal and refers to a filing period of October 1–December 1, with no year on that sentence. UC Irvine’s own TAG page for fall 2027 says October 1–November 30, 2026. This page could not confirm that December 1 applies to fall 2027. Use November 30 unless the campus confirms a later date.
  • Transfer Academic Update: opens December 15, 2026. Priority deadline January 31, 2027. UC Santa Cruz says transfer students must submit a TAU even if nothing changed.
  • FAFSA or California Dream Act Application, and the Cal Grant GPA verification: filing opens October 1, 2026 and the dates page lists March 2, 2027.
  • Fall 2027 admission notifications begin March 1, 2027, on the system dates page. Campuses post on their own schedules. UC Santa Barbara says April. UC Riverside says early April for the official admission decision after a TAG review. UC Santa Cruz lists April 1–30, 2027 for on-time transfer decisions.
  • Next TAG window on the dates page: May 1–31, 2027, for winter/spring 2028. On the TAG matrix, UC Merced is the campus that lists a spring TAG in that May window. Davis, Irvine, Riverside, Santa Barbara, and Santa Cruz are listed for fall only.

Pick a campus

Irvine, Santa Barbara, and Santa Cruz are written out in full. Davis, Merced, and Riverside follow from their admissions pages and the TAG matrix. With JavaScript off, every campus stays on the page.

UC Irvine

The TAG matrix says Irvine does not pre-evaluate coursework as part of TAG. Irvine’s page says missing or dropping any TAG rule voids the guarantee. The same page says many students who are not eligible for TAG are still encouraged to apply through the regular UC application, October 1–November 30, 2026.

Recheck

  • Minimum 3.4 UC-transferable GPA by the end of fall 2026, and maintain at least 3.4. Also a 2.0 with no grade below C in fall, winter, and spring coursework before enrollment.
  • By the end of fall 2026: one UC-M and one UC-E with a C or better, and completed major prerequisites must already meet that major’s grades and GPA. By the end of spring 2027: the second UC-E, the four-course pattern, 60 semester units (90 quarter), and all major prerequisites.
  • At submission: 30 semester (45 quarter) UC-transferable units, which can include AP, IB, or A-level credit. Irvine also lists the shared limits: 30 semester units at a California community college, last regular term at a community college, no more than 19 semester units (28.5 quarter) in the last term to reach 60, and the 80 semester unit cap if you attended a school other than a community college.
  • The TAG major and the primary major on the UC application must match exactly. Irvine says you cannot change the TAG major after you submit the TAG, and you cannot change the primary major after you submit the UC application. A different primary major nullifies the TAG. Irvine says students who are not eligible for TAG are still encouraged to apply through the regular UC application.
  • Fall 2027 majors excluded from TAG: Art; Business Administration; Dance; Music, B.Mus.; Nursing Science; all majors in the Donald Bren School of Information and Computer Sciences. For fall 2028, Irvine lists more excluded majors, including Biological Sciences, Economics, Psychology, and several engineering majors. That list is on Irvine’s TAG page and in the matrix notes.

Reasons Irvine lists for a TAG that does not stand

  • Still in high school at submission; a bachelor’s, graduate, or professional degree; prior enrollment at UC Irvine in a regular term (summer excluded); not in good standing at any UC; missing the 30 community college units; last term not at a community college; more than 19 semester units needed in the last term; over the 80-unit cap after limits if you attended a non-community-college; a required course still in progress to repair a deficient grade; a prior falsified UC application; dismissal, suspension, or separation for student conduct. Also: incomplete or inaccurate coursework on the TAG or UC application; missing the January 31, 2027 TAU; no reply to a request for information by the deadline Irvine states; a different primary major.

Appeal and contact

Irvine’s TAG page does not describe an appeal that restores a voided TAG. It tells students who are not eligible to apply in the regular process. A separate appeals page is for an admission denial or a late application. It asks for new information, through the UC Irvine Applicant Portal, and says letters of recommendation are not considered. The transfer selection dates on that page are May 15 to file and July 1 for a decision, with no admission year printed next to them. Do not treat those dates as confirmed for fall 2027 until Irvine’s page shows the year.

Contact: Office of Undergraduate Admissions, 260 Aldrich Hall, Irvine, CA 92697-1075, (949) 824-6703. The TAG matrix lists the same phone number.

UC Santa Barbara

Santa Barbara does not give an early TAG decision. The campus waits for the UC application and the January TAU, then posts TAG and non-TAG decisions together in April. That decision is final for schedule or grade changes made after it. If you are not TAG-eligible, Santa Barbara says you are still evaluated and considered for admission.

Recheck

  • Minimum 3.40 UC-transferable GPA by the end of fall 2026. No grade lower than C in fall 2026, winter 2027, or spring 2027, and good academic standing. If you attended a UC, you must be in good standing there.
  • 30 semester (45 quarter) units by the end of summer 2026. By spring 2027: second UC-E, four courses from at least two of UC-H, UC-B, and UC-S, and 60 semester (90 quarter) units, with at least 30 semester (45 quarter) at a community college and no more than 19 units in spring 2027 to reach 60. Last regular term at a California community college.
  • By the end of fall 2026: one UC-E and one UC-M with a C or better. Selective majors in Biological Sciences, Chemistry and Biochemistry, Economics, Mathematics, Physics, and Statistics and Applied Probability also need the major-preparation GPA by the end of fall 2026 and the required major courses by the end of spring 2027. Santa Barbara says to read the “By Major” agreement on ASSIST. Other majors can be TAG majors without every ASSIST course finished. Santa Barbara still recommends finishing as much major preparation as you can.
  • UC application October 1–November 30, 2026, for the same major, listed as the first-choice major. TAU by January 31, 2027, and reply to UC requests. The UC application is how Santa Barbara says to correct a TAG mistake. Winter and spring courses may differ from the TAG if you still meet eligibility and major preparation.
  • Changing the major voids the TAG. Santa Barbara says you are then reviewed for regular admission to the major on the UC application. If the major change is the only miss, and you have the required preparation for the new major, the campus says you are a strong candidate. That is not a guarantee.
  • No TAG for the College of Creative Studies, the College of Engineering, or Dance B.A., Dance B.F.A., Music B.M., Theater B.A. (Theater Design emphasis), or Theater B.F.A. (Acting). Some Letters and Science majors admit to a pre-major. The pre-major can be the guarantee; the full major comes after further coursework at Santa Barbara. TAG does not apply at 80 semester (120 quarter) units from a four-year university, or from a four-year university plus community colleges. IGETC or Cal-GETC is not required for the TAG.

Appeal and contact

There is no separate “TAG appeal” on the TAG page, because Santa Barbara does not approve or deny the TAG before the April admission decision. The appeal page is for an admission decision. It says very few students are admitted on appeal. The appeal has to add information that was not in the application. Improved fall grades or new activities are not enough. Transfer applicants need a transcript. If the Appeal tab is in the UCSB Applicant Portal, the page lists May 1 as the open date and June 15 as the transfer deadline, with no year printed on those dates. Email, mail, and fax are not reviewed. Waitlisted students appeal only after the waitlist ends. Contact: Undergraduate Admissions, 1210 Cheadle Hall, Santa Barbara, CA 93106-2014, (805) 893-2881.

UC Santa Cruz

Santa Cruz posts the TAG decision in the UC Transfer Admission Planner on or after November 15. The campus says TAG decisions are final and appeals will not be considered. It also says you may still be competitive for regular admission without the TAG, and it tells you to apply in the fall filing period or for a later term.

Recheck

  • Santa Cruz’s TAG page requires a 3.0 overall UC-transferable GPA at the time you submit the TAG, plus 30 semester (45 quarter) units. The TAG matrix states the 3.0 by the end of summer 2026. Both pages are official. If they differ for your record, use the campus page and ask Santa Cruz.
  • By the end of fall before transfer: the first English composition course and the math course. By the end of spring: the rest of the seven-course pattern, 60 semester (90 quarter) units, at least 30 semester (45 quarter) at a community college, required major preparation with the required grades, no more than 19 units in that spring term to reach junior standing, good standing, and no UC-transferable grade below C after the TAG.
  • Screening majors add major-preparation rules. Santa Cruz says those courses and the major selection criteria are due by the end of spring, and it lists them on the TAG page. The matrix says TAG is open for all majors and points to that page for screening majors. Do not guess the GPA for a screening major from the 3.0 campus minimum.
  • The UC application major must match the TAG major. A different major can void the guarantee. Summer cannot be used to finish courses or units the TAG required by an earlier term. Course changes go on the UC application and the January TAU. Santa Cruz says both are used in the admission decision.
  • Not eligible: more than 80 semester (120 quarter) units of combined lower- and upper-division work after UC limits, unless you only attended a California community college; a former UC student under 2.0 at that UC; a former UC Santa Cruz student (readmission instead); a bachelor’s degree or higher; current high school enrollment.

Appeal and contact

Santa Cruz’s TAG decision page says no appeal of the TAG decision will be considered. If you think the decision is wrong, the campus says to submit the UC application in the filing period, or for a later term, and to use the comment field to explain the mistake. It also says to read the TAG requirements with your community college counselor. You can submit a later TAG in the UC Transfer Admission Planner; criteria change annually and Santa Cruz says new criteria are posted in mid-July. A separate admissions appeal page covers an admission denial, not the TAG decision. Contact: (831) 459-4008. The TAG matrix lists the same phone number and this email: tag@ucsc.edu

UC Davis

Davis releases the TAG decision in the UC Transfer Admission Planner on or after November 15. The matrix says to wait for that decision before you submit the UC application. If the TAG is not approved, Davis says the decision is final and an appeal is not considered. Davis also says you may still be competitive in regular admission, and to file the UC application in the filing period or for a later term. Use the comment field if you think the TAG decision is a mistake.

  • GPA by the end of summer 2026: 3.20 for Agricultural and Environmental Sciences, Biological Sciences, and Letters and Science; 3.50 overall and in major preparation for Engineering. Selective majors add their own GPA. No grade lower than C in UC-transferable work for the selective-major rule, and no grade lower than C in the current fall, winter, and spring terms.
  • Davis says IGETC is not required for the TAG. Business, Computer Science, and Data Science are not TAG majors. Undeclared programs are excluded. Landscape Architecture is a pre-major, with no guarantee to the major. The matrix and the Davis requirements page are the GPA sources.
  • A later TAG is allowed. Update the same Transfer Admission Planner, including the term. Davis says criteria can change each May and that new criteria are available in mid-May. If you cannot meet an approved TAG, Davis says to apply through the regular transfer application. Many students are admitted that way. That is Davis’s wording, not a promise.
  • Contact: (530) 752-2971, and the advisor lookup on the Davis admissions site. The TAG matrix lists both.

UC Merced

The TAG matrix lists a fall TAG decision date of November 15 and says to wait for the decision before the UC application. Merced’s TAG page says the same: students with an approved TAG must file the UC application for that major and term, and Merced encourages you to wait for the decision. This page did not find a Merced sentence that says a denied TAG is reviewed as a regular application. The system matrix still says students without a TAG apply during the regular filing period. For a Merced-specific reading of your denial, use the contact below.

  • GPA on the matrix, by the end of summer 2026: Engineering 3.0; Natural Sciences 2.9; Social Sciences, Humanities and Arts 2.8, with Psychology and Public Health at 3.0. Merced’s TAG page lists the same school minimums. Public Health TAG requires IGETC certification. The matrix says IGETC or Cal-GETC is required for Public Health only.
  • Merced is the matrix campus that accepts a spring TAG, filing period May 1–31. Spring 2028 course deadlines are on the last pages of the matrix. Fall filing on the Merced page is September 1–30.
  • At the time the TAG is submitted, Merced’s TAG page requires full-time enrollment at a California community college. Students who meet all TAG requirements but are not enrolled full-time may still apply if they provide an explanation in the gap in education section.
  • Merced’s appeals page is for an admission denial, a late application, a late SIR, or a rescinded offer. It does not say it restores a TAG. Questions about TAG: (209) 228-7178, or this email. transfer@ucmerced.edu The matrix lists both. Merced also points students to its contact page to schedule a transfer adviser.

UC Riverside

Riverside says you are notified of TAG approval after mid-November, and the matrix says to wait for that decision before the UC application. If you applied for TAG and are ineligible, Riverside says you are still evaluated and considered for regular admission. If you miss any TAG condition, admission is not guaranteed. Changing the major voids the TAG; Riverside says you are then reviewed for regular admission to the major on the UC application.

  • GPA on Riverside’s TAG page, before you submit the TAG: Engineering, Computer Science 3.60, Computer Engineering 3.30, Mechanical Engineering 3.10, other engineering 3.00; Humanities, Arts, and Social Sciences, Psychology and Theatre, Film, and Digital Production 3.30, other majors 2.80; Natural and Agricultural Sciences 2.80; Business 2.80; Education 2.70; Public Policy 2.70. The June 9, 2026 matrix matches those figures and adds two exceptions not repeated on Riverside’s TAG page as checked: Data Science 3.0 in Natural and Agricultural Sciences, and Computer Science w/ Business Applications 3.3 in Engineering, both required by the end of summer 2026. Use both pages before relying on 2.80 for Data Science or 3.00 for that computer science major.
  • Riverside’s TAG page requires at least one regular academic term at a community college after high-school graduation and before TAG submission.
  • Riverside’s TAG page says Art (Studio) and Education, Society, and Human Development with Teaching Credential are closed for TAG. The matrix lists Art Studio. Selective majors need major preparation by the end of spring, with no grade below C. IGETC or Cal-GETC is not required. The matrix says the School of Business highly recommends Cal-GETC or IGETC.
  • The UC application between October 1 and November 30 is how Riverside says to correct a TAG mistake, with the TAU in January. Official admission decisions for an approved TAG are in early April, after the conditions are met. This page did not find a Riverside page that calls a TAG denial itself appealable. Questions: (951) 827-3411, or the ask form. admissions.ucr.edu/ask

If this term is not the one

  • File the UC application by November 30, 2026 if you still want fall 2027 to be reviewed. One application can include the TAG campus and other campuses. UC Berkeley, UCLA, and UC San Diego do not offer TAG; they are still on the same application.
  • Davis and Santa Cruz both tell students whose TAG was not approved to talk with a community college counselor and to decide between this filing period and a later term. Both say you can submit a TAG for a later term in the UC Transfer Admission Planner.
  • The UC TAG overview lists September 1–30 as the fall TAG filing window and May 1–31 as the winter/spring TAG filing window. The dated page for this cycle already closed the fall 2027 TAG on September 30, 2026. It lists the next TAG filing as May 1–31, 2027, for winter/spring 2028. Only Merced’s matrix row says a spring TAG is accepted.
  • Before a later TAG, recheck the GPA, the seven-course pattern, major preparation on ASSIST, and the campus page. Davis says TAG criteria can change in May. Santa Cruz says new criteria are posted in mid-July. Major preparation on ASSIST is what Transfer-OS compares for the colleges and majors it covers. It does not decide a TAG.
