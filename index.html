FAQ

Is UCPlan official?

No. UCPlan is an independent project. It is not affiliated with the University of California or with ASSIST. Transfer data comes from public ASSIST reports.

Does UCPlan guarantee admission?

No. UCPlan is a planning aid. Admission decisions belong to the UC campuses. Nothing here promises or predicts an admission outcome.

Which UC campuses offer TAG?

Six campuses participate in the Transfer Admission Guarantee (TAG): UC Davis, UC Irvine, UC Merced, UC Riverside, UC Santa Barbara, and UC Santa Cruz. UCLA, UC Berkeley, and UC San Diego do not offer TAG.

What does UCPlan cost?

Nothing right now. The free tools stay free. A Pro plan later will add new features only; current features will not be taken away.

Do I need an account?

No. The free tools work without signing up. An account system will arrive with Pro.

Which colleges are covered?

Transfer-OS covers 50 California community colleges × 6 UC campuses. UC Course Planner covers programs at UC Irvine, UC San Diego, and UC Santa Barbara, and coverage keeps growing.
