How we update the data

  • Source: public ASSIST articulation agreements (assist.org). No private data.
  • Refreshed when ASSIST publishes a new academic year. The data in the tools was pulled from ASSIST on November 2, 2026.
  • Before each release we compare the new data with the previous release (colleges, majors and data years added or removed) and spot-check sample reports against ASSIST.

FAQ

Is UCPlan official?

No. UCPlan is an independent project. It is not affiliated with the University of California or with ASSIST. Transfer data comes from public ASSIST reports.

Does UCPlan guarantee admission?

No. UCPlan is a planning aid. Admission decisions belong to the UC campuses. Nothing here promises or predicts an admission outcome.

Which UC campuses offer TAG?

Six campuses participate in the Transfer Admission Guarantee (TAG): UC Davis, UC Irvine, UC Merced, UC Riverside, UC Santa Barbara, and UC Santa Cruz. UCLA, UC Berkeley, and UC San Diego do not offer TAG.

Transfer-OS has agreement data for 50 California community colleges, 6 UC campuses (UC Berkeley, UCLA, UC San Diego, UC Davis, UC Irvine, and UC Santa Cruz), and 108 UC majors. Not every college has data for every campus and year. UC Course Planner is for students already at UC and covers only UC Irvine, UC San Diego, and UC Santa Barbara (22 majors).

Do I need IGETC or Cal-GETC to transfer to UC?

No. Neither is an admission requirement. UC asks for a seven-course pattern by the end of the spring term before you transfer: two English composition courses, one math or quantitative reasoning course, and four courses from at least two of arts and humanities, social and behavioral sciences, and physical and biological sciences — all UC-transferable. You also need 60 semester units and a 2.4 GPA in UC-transferable courses (2.8 if you are a nonresident). If you finish IGETC or Cal-GETC you may already have met the seven-course pattern, and some majors recommend it, so check ASSIST and your transfer center.

UC Admissions: basic requirements and the seven-course pattern

How much is the UC application? Is there a fee waiver?

$80 per UC campus ($95 for international and non-immigrant applicants). UC waives the fee for up to four campuses for eligible U.S. citizens, permanent residents and AB 540 students; the application tells you whether you qualify once you enter family size and income. Fees are not refundable.

UC Admissions: application fees and waivers

What about financial aid (FAFSA or Dream Act)?

File the 2027–28 FAFSA, or the California Dream Act Application if you are an undocumented student, between October 1, 2026 and March 2, 2027. California residents (and AB 540 students) also need a Cal Grant GPA verification; California community colleges usually submit it for you, but check with your school.

UC Admissions: dates and deadlines

Can I transfer in winter or spring?

Most UC campuses admit transfer students for fall only. Select campuses accept winter/spring applications in a July filing period (July 1–31 for winter/spring 2027). Not every campus or major is open, so check the campus before you apply. TAG for winter/spring has its own filing period, May 1–31.

Who should I talk to about my own plan?

Your community college transfer center or counselor. They know your college’s ASSIST agreements and deadlines, and they can review your plan with you. UCPlan does not give personal transfer advising.
