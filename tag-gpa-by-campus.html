TAG GPA by campus

This page lists the minimum UC-transferable GPA required for TAG, by campus. Meeting a minimum does not guarantee admission. TAG also requires the shared criteria and campus major preparation.

The fall 2027 TAG filing period closed on September 30, 2026. You can still submit the regular UC application by November 30, 2026. A TAG is not the application. Official dates: UC Admissions: Dates & deadlines and the UC TAG overview.

UC Davis

Required by the end of summer 2026:

  • Agricultural and Environmental Sciences: 3.2
  • Biological Sciences: 3.2
  • Letters and Science: 3.2
  • Engineering: 3.5

Each selective major also has specific major-preparation GPA requirements.

UC Irvine

Minimum UC-transferable GPA of 3.4 by the end of fall 2026. Major prerequisites are required for specific majors.

UC Merced

Required by the end of summer 2026:

  • Engineering: 3.0
  • Natural Sciences: 2.9
  • Social Sciences, Humanities and Arts: 2.8

Exceptions: Psychology: 3.0; Public Health: 3.0.

UC Riverside

Required by the end of summer 2026:

  • Humanities, Arts, and Social Sciences: 2.8 (exceptions: Psychology: 3.3; Theater, Film & Digital Production: 3.3)
  • Bourns Engineering: 3.0 (exceptions: Computer Science: 3.6; Computer Science with Business Applications: 3.3; Computer Engineering: 3.3; Mechanical Engineering: 3.1)
  • Natural and Agricultural Sciences: 2.8 (exception: Data Science: 3.0)
  • Business: 2.8
  • Education: 2.7
  • Public Policy: 2.7

UC Santa Barbara

Minimum UC-transferable GPA of 3.4 by the end of fall 2026 for all majors. A minimum major-preparation GPA is also required for Biological Sciences, Chemistry and Biochemistry, Economics, Mathematics, Physics, and Statistics and Applied Probability.

UC Santa Cruz

Minimum UC-transferable GPA of 3.0 by the end of summer 2026 for all majors. Additional major-preparation GPA requirements apply for some (screening) majors.

Source

Source: UC Transfer Admission Guarantee (TAG) matrix, accurate as of June 9, 2026, for the fall 2027 and spring 2028 TAG application cycle. Subject to change.

A note for fall 2028

For fall 2028 admission, UC Irvine will exclude additional majors from TAG (including Biological Sciences, Economics, Psychology, and several engineering majors).
