UC transfer application deadline for fall 2027

The official filing window is October 1 through November 30, 2026. A TAG is not the UC application. Dates below are copied from UC Admissions pages, with links so you can check them yourself.

The application window

For fall 2027 admission, the general UC application filing period is October 1 through November 30, 2026. The application itself opens on August 1, but it has to be submitted during that October–November window.

Source: UC Admissions: Dates & deadlines and UC Admissions: Applying as a transfer.

A TAG is not the UC application

A Transfer Admission Guarantee (TAG) is a separate filing from the regular UC application. The fall 2027 TAG filing period was September 1–30, 2026, and it has closed.

If you already filed a TAG, you still have to submit the regular UC application between October 1 and November 30. If you missed the TAG deadline, you can still apply to UC during that same window.

Source: UC Admissions: Transfer Admission Guarantee (TAG). Campus GPA minimums are listed on our TAG GPA by campus page, taken from the official TAG matrix.

After you apply: the Transfer Academic Update

The Transfer Academic Update (TAU) opens December 15, 2026. The priority deadline is January 31, 2027. UC uses it so you can report final fall grades and in-progress or planned coursework.

Source: UC Admissions: Dates & deadlines and UC Admissions: Transfer academic update.
