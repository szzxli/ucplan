What UCPlan does for your students

  • Transfer-OS: agreement data for 50 California community colleges, 6 UC campuses, and 108 UC majors. Students pick their college, target UC, and major, and see what transfers and what is still missing. Open Transfer-OS
  • UC Course Planner: graduation planning for 22 majors at UC Irvine, UC San Diego, and UC Santa Barbara, for students already at UC. Open UC Course Planner
  • TAG GPA by campus: the minimum TAG GPA for each of the six TAG campuses (Davis, Irvine, Merced, Riverside, Santa Barbara, and Santa Cruz). TAG GPA by campus
  • TAG not approved: a checklist for a student whose TAG was not approved. TAG checklist
  • UC transfer deadline: the application deadline for UC transfer. Transfer deadline

What it does not do

  • UCPlan is unofficial. It is not affiliated with the University of California or with ASSIST.
  • It does not replace ASSIST.org or a counselor. It is a planning aid.
  • Nothing on this site guarantees or predicts admission.
  • Data years are labeled: ASSIST data was pulled on November 2, 2026, and the coverage page lists the data year for each UC campus.

Free, no sign-up

UCPlan is free and works without signing up. Page views are counted with GoatCounter, a cookie-free counter; no personal data is collected.

Contact

Questions about the tools? Email info@ucplan.org. — David Lee. Support covers tool problems only, not personal transfer advising.
