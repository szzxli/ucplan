Irvine Valley College → UC Irvine Psychology, B.S. transfer courses

If you take these courses at Irvine Valley College, this is how they transfer to UC Irvine Psychology, B.S. under the ASSIST articulation agreement for 2026-2027, published 2026-11-05. Unofficial — verify with ASSIST before you plan around it.

16 of 19 UC Irvine course requirements in this agreement have articulated Irvine Valley College courses.

3 UC Irvine course requirement(s) have no articulated Irvine Valley College course (COGS 9B, SOC SCI 3A, COGS 10A + COGS 10B + COGS 10C).
