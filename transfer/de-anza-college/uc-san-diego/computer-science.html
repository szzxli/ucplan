De Anza College → UC San Diego CSE: Computer Science B.S. transfer courses

If you take these courses at De Anza College, this is how they transfer to UC San Diego CSE: Computer Science B.S. under the ASSIST articulation agreement for 2026-2027, published 2026-11-23. Unofficial — verify with ASSIST before you plan around it.

Course articulations

14 of 15 UC San Diego course requirements in this agreement have articulated De Anza College courses.

1 UC San Diego course requirement(s) have no articulated De Anza College course (CSE 30).
