De Anza College → UCLA Economics/B.A. transfer courses

If you take these courses at De Anza College, this is how they transfer to UCLA Economics/B.A. under the ASSIST articulation agreement for 2025-2026, published 2026-06-10. Unofficial — verify with ASSIST before you plan around it.

Course articulations

4 of 6 UCLA course requirements in this agreement have articulated De Anza College courses.

2 UCLA course requirement(s) have no articulated De Anza College course (ECON 11, ECON 41).
