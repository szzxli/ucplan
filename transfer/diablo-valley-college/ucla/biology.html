Diablo Valley College → UCLA Biology/B.S. transfer courses

If you take these courses at Diablo Valley College, this is how they transfer to UCLA Biology/B.S. under the ASSIST articulation agreement for 2025-2026, published 2026-04-29. Unofficial — verify with ASSIST before you plan around it.

Course articulations

14 of 17 UCLA course requirements in this agreement have articulated Diablo Valley College courses.

3 UCLA course requirement(s) have no articulated Diablo Valley College course (MATH 3C, LIFESCI 40, CHEM 153A).
