Diablo Valley College → UCLA Psychology/B.A. transfer courses

22 of 25 UCLA course requirements in this agreement have articulated Diablo Valley College courses.

3 UCLA course requirement(s) have no articulated Diablo Valley College course (PHILOS 3, PHILOS 23, PHILOS 31).
