Diablo Valley College → UC Berkeley Psychology, B.A. transfer courses

17 of 19 UC Berkeley course requirements in this agreement have articulated Diablo Valley College courses.

2 UC Berkeley course requirement(s) have no articulated Diablo Valley College course (STAT 21, STAT 20).
