Diablo Valley College → UC San Diego CSE: Computer Science B.S. transfer courses

If you take these courses at Diablo Valley College, this is how they transfer to UC San Diego CSE: Computer Science B.S. under the ASSIST articulation agreement for 2026-2027, published 2026-11-13. Unofficial — verify with ASSIST before you plan around it.

How to read this table

  • This page is a planning reference, not an admission decision.
