Santa Barbara City College → UC Davis Computer Science B.S. transfer courses

If you take these courses at Santa Barbara City College, this is how they transfer to UC Davis Computer Science B.S. under the ASSIST articulation agreement for 2025-2026, published 2026-05-12. Unofficial — verify with ASSIST before you plan around it.

16 of 17 UC Davis course requirements in this agreement have articulated Santa Barbara City College courses.

1 UC Davis course requirement(s) have no articulated Santa Barbara City College course (ECS 036B).

How to read this table

  • Each row is one UC Davis course requirement. Santa Barbara City College courses joined by + must all be taken; courses joined by or are alternatives. Parentheses group courses that belong together.
  • Notes are quoted from the ASSIST agreement. A note can change what a course satisfies — read it before you plan around the row.
  • This page is a planning reference, not an admission decision.
