Santa Barbara City College → UC San Diego CSE: Computer Science B.S. transfer courses

How to read this table

  • Each row is one UC San Diego course requirement. Santa Barbara City College courses joined by + must all be taken; courses joined by or are alternatives. Parentheses group courses that belong together.
  • Notes are quoted from the ASSIST agreement. A note can change what a course satisfies — read it before you plan around the row.
  • This page is a planning reference, not an admission decision.
