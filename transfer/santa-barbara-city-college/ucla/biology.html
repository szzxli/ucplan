Santa Barbara City College → UCLA Biology/B.S. transfer courses

If you take these courses at Santa Barbara City College, this is how they transfer to UCLA Biology/B.S. under the ASSIST articulation agreement for 2025-2026, published 2026-03-06. Unofficial — verify with ASSIST before you plan around it.

Course articulations

12 of 16 UCLA course requirements in this agreement have articulated Santa Barbara City College courses.

4 UCLA course requirement(s) have no articulated Santa Barbara City College course (MATH 3A, MATH 3B, MATH 3C, CHEM 153A).

How to read this table

  • Each row is one UCLA course requirement. Santa Barbara City College courses joined by + must all be taken; courses joined by or are alternatives. Parentheses group courses that belong together.
  • Notes are quoted from the ASSIST agreement. A note can change what a course satisfies — read it before you plan around the row.
  • This page is a planning reference, not an admission decision.
