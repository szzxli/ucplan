What you get

  • A semester-by-semester course plan for your major.
  • Prerequisite order, so you never plan a course before you are ready for it.
  • General education and major requirements shown together, not in separate lists.
  • Programs at UC Irvine, UC San Diego, and UC Santa Barbara, with more on the way.

How it fits with Transfer-OS

Transfer-OS answers “where am I going, and what is missing?” UC Course Planner answers “what do I take, and when?” Use them in that order: path first, semesters second.

Free, no account needed

UC Course Planner is free and works without signing up.

The two tools do not share data yet. Enter your college, target UC, and major in each tool separately.
