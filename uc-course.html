UC Course Planner: graduation planning for students already at UC.

Plan the semesters you have left toward your degree — prerequisites respected, general education and major requirements lined up.

Open UC Course Planner ← Transfer-OS (before you transfer)

Who it is for

Students who are already at UC Irvine, UC San Diego or UC Santa Barbara. Its first question is whether you are aiming for grad school or a job; then it plans your semesters against your graduation requirements. It is not a transfer planner — if you are still at a community college, start with Transfer-OS.

What you get

  • A semester-by-semester course plan for your major.
  • Prerequisite order, so you never plan a course before you are ready for it.
  • General education and major requirements shown together, not in separate lists.
  • Your plan is saved only in this browser.

How it fits with Transfer-OS

Transfer-OS is for the years before you transfer; UC Course Planner is for the years after. If you are at a community college, start with Transfer-OS.

Free, no account needed

UC Course Planner is free and works without signing up.

The two tools do not share data yet. Enter your college, target UC, and major in each tool separately.
